Thesis
Student eWallet turns scattered school-payment tasks into a student-centered wallet, with verified student identity, wallet operations, internal-service payment, spending analytics, savings jars, and admin control.

Audience
Graduation thesis committee.

One-line arc
From school-payment friction to a working multi-surface prototype: iOS app for students, Express/MongoDB backend, admin web console, VNPay-assisted top-up flow, and AI spending guidance.
